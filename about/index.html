Hey there! I’m Pranav Kalpathi Shankar, a high school student at Westwood High School and an aspiring aerospace engineer with a passion for bringing ambitious ideas to life through engineering, leadership, and design.

From 2024 to 2026, I led our TARC (Team America Rocketry Challenge) team, guiding us through every phase from concept to launch. I also served as the Team Lead for our Technology Student Association team, where I helped us qualify for the national competition in Nashville. On the robotics side, I was the CAD Lead for FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) Team 17264 ‘Arrowhead’, designing and iterating precision components to boost performance on the field.

I thrive at the intersection of creativity and technical challenge—whether I’m engineering a rocket’s trajectory or collaborating on a robot’s drivetrain. Aerospace is the future, and I’m determined to be part of shaping it.
